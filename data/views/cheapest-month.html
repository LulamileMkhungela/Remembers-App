The cheapest month to fly Cape Town to London

March is the cheapest month for this route, with fares starting at R9 420 one way. Fly midweek and it drops again.

Prices sampled over 90 days · one way, economy, hand luggage

Most people book December because that is when they want to travel. That is also precisely when the route is at its most expensive, which is why the same seat costs three times as much in the last week of December.

A Tuesday departure is usually about R900 cheaper than a Friday on the same week.

One stop is normally cheaper than a direct flight on this route, and the difference is large enough that the extra hours are worth counting carefully. One stop via Istanbul came in at R9 420 for the dates we sampled.

If your dates are loose, the trick is to set a price alert rather than checking the site every day. Alerts fire on the route and the month, not on a specific day, so a cheap Tuesday will find you.
